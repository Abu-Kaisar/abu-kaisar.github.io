🧑‍🎓 Career Summary

Abu Kaisar Md Faisal is a Lecturer in Mechanical Engineering at Khwaja Yunus Ali University (KYAU), Bangladesh, and a researcher affiliated with the Advanced Fluid Flow Research Group (AFFRG), RUET. His research focuses on Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), biomimetic aerodynamics, aerodynamic optimization, and nature-inspired engineering design.

His work integrates numerical simulations, experimental wind tunnel investigations, and optimization techniques to enhance aerodynamic performance under pre-stall and post-stall conditions.

He has contributed to multiple peer-reviewed journal articles in internationally recognized journals including Physics of Fluids, Ocean Engineering, Journal of Cleaner Production, and Environmental Science and Pollution Research.

Current research interests include stall-adaptive airfoils, vortex dynamics, low-speed aerodynamics, vertical-axis wind turbines, and bio-inspired flow control systems.

📑 Selected Projects

  • Stall-Adaptive Biomimetic Airfoil: Numerical and experimental optimization of dimpled and leading-edge protuberance surfaces for enhanced pre-stall and post-stall aerodynamic performance.
  • Corrugated Airfoil Optimization: Bio-inspired aerodynamic enhancement study using surrogate modeling, Response Surface Methodology (RSM), and CFD simulations.
  • Vertical Axis Wind Turbine Airfoil Blending: Development of blended airfoil configurations to improve low-speed aerodynamic efficiency of VAWTs.
  • Slocum Underwater Glider Hydrodynamics: CFD-based drag reduction and hydrodynamic performance analysis of underwater glider configurations.
