🧑‍🎓 Career Summary & Research Vision

Abu Kaisar Md Faisal is a Lecturer in Mechanical Engineering at Khwaja Yunus Ali University (KYAU), Bangladesh, and an active researcher at the Computational Flow Physics & Experimental Aerodynamics (CFPEA) Lab, Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), under the supervision of Dr. Mahadi Hasan Masud.

His research integrates high-fidelity numerical simulations (CFD), surrogate modeling (RSM & Genetic Algorithms), and subsonic wind tunnel validation to design next-generation aerodynamic systems. He investigates how nature-inspired surface modifications—such as humpback whale tubercle protuberances, penguin wing kinematics, and corrugated dragonfly cross-sections—can suppress boundary layer separation and postpone stall.

He has contributed to 9 peer-reviewed journal publications, 4 book chapters with Elsevier and Springer Nature, and 3 international conference proceedings, with research appearing in top-tier journals including Physics of Fluids, Ocean Engineering, and Journal of Cleaner Production.

🔍 Research Interests & Core Themes

🌪️

Biomimetic Aerodynamics

Leading-edge protuberances, tubercle-modified airfoils, corrugated wing sections, and vortex generators inspired by marine biology and avian kinematics.

💻

Computational Fluid Dynamics

High-resolution RANS (SST $k-\omega$, Transition SST) and URANS simulations utilizing ANSYS Fluent, CFX, and OpenFOAM running on HPC clusters.

📈

Aerodynamic Optimization

Design of Experiments (DOE), Response Surface Methodology (RSM), Genetic Algorithms, and surrogate modeling for multi-objective aerodynamic design.

💨

Wind Turbine Aerodynamics

Airfoil blending and flow control for low-speed Vertical Axis Wind Turbines (VAWTs) to elevate starting torque and aerodynamic power coefficients.

🌊

Marine Hydrodynamics

Hydrodynamic drag reduction, hull form modifications, and flow visualization for autonomous Slocum underwater gliders.

♻️

Sustainable Energy & E-Waste

Material flow analysis, environmental risk assessment of e-waste recycling, and municipal solid waste-to-energy conversion systems.
